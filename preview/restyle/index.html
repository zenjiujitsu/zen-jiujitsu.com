Questions

No experience needed. Honest answers before a first visit.

Jiu-jitsu

Do I need any experience to start?

None whatsoever. Everyone at Zen started as a beginner. Our adult intro classes on Friday and Saturday/Sunday Open Mats are specifically designed for people walking in for the first time.

Do I need to be in shape before I start?

No. Jiu-Jitsu gets you in shape, you don't need to be in shape to do it. Most people are surprised by how much of a workout it is, and how quickly their fitness improves just from showing up consistently.

What should I wear to my first class?

Athletic shorts and a t-shirt are perfectly fine for your first visit. No gi (uniform) required until you decide to join.

Is it safe? I'm worried about getting hurt.

Jiu-Jitsu is one of the safest martial arts because it's largely ground-based with no striking. Our coaches prioritize safe training above everything, tapping out is respected, egos are checked at the door.

How does the free trial class work?

Book online, show up, and train, no charge. After your trial, we'll walk you through membership options and get you started on the right plan. No pressure, no hard sell.

How much does membership cost?

Memberships start at $109/month and go up to $289/month depending on frequency (4x, 8x, or unlimited) and whether bathhouse access is included. We'd rather walk you through the options after your free class so we can match you to the right plan.

Can I just drop in without a membership?

Yes. A day pass is $50 and gives you access to every Jiu-Jitsu class running that day. Add a 60-minute bathhouse session for $75 total. Book online anytime.

What's the difference between Gi and No‑Gi?

Gi training uses a traditional uniform which adds grips and lapel work. No‑Gi is done in shorts and a rash guard, faster and more wrestling-influenced. Both are offered at Zen, and most members train both.

Youth program

Can my 3-year-old really do this?

Yes, our Ninjas class is built specifically for ages 3–4. It's play-based, movement-focused, and structured around what kids that age actually respond to.

Will my child get hurt?

Safety is the first priority in every youth class. Our coaches are trained specifically to work with kids, and all contact is supervised and age-appropriate.

How soon will I see changes in my child?

Most parents notice changes within the first 4–8 weeks, better focus, more confidence in social situations, and calmer behavior at home. By 3 months, most kids are transformed.

What if my child is shy or nervous?

That's completely normal, and honestly, those kids often benefit the most. Our coaches are patient, low-pressure, and great at drawing shy kids out at their own pace.

The bathhouse

Do I need to be a Jiu-Jitsu member to use the bathhouse?

Not at all. The bathhouse is open to everyone, you can book a session completely independently of any Jiu-Jitsu membership.

Can I book just the cold plunge?

The bathhouse is built around contrast therapy — moving between hot and cold — so we don't sell amenities à la carte. But you're welcome to book a session and use just the cold plunge if that's all you're after. You get the full hour either way.

What should I bring for my first visit?

A towel and sandals or slippers are required and not provided, so bring your own. Bring a bathing suit too — suits can be rented for $5 while supplies last. A shower is required before entering, and we provide body wash and shampoo. Arrive about 15 minutes early. Robes and locks are optional, available to rent or buy.

How long is each session?

Each session is 1 hour, which is enough time for 2–3 full contrast rounds. Sessions are by appointment so you always have the space to yourself or with your group.

Are you open in winter, rain, or snow?

Yes — we're open year-round. The outdoor space has infrared heaters and louvered pergolas, and soaking or plunging in the rain or snow is honestly one of the best parts. The only exception is thunder or lightning, when we clear the outdoor space for safety and reschedule your visit, no questions asked.

I've never done a cold plunge. Is it safe?

For most healthy adults, yes. We recommend starting with shorter exposures (30–60 seconds) and building up gradually. If you have cardiovascular conditions or are pregnant, consult your doctor first.
